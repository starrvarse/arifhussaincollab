About Arif Hussain

A developer who runs the businesses he writes software for.

I'm Arif Hussain, a software developer and founder from Assam, India. I've been writing software for eight years, and in 2023 I started BigSur Corp in Guwahati to turn that work into products: a self-hosted business suite, a multi-tenant ERP, offline billing and accounting, and a data platform.

I like owning the whole stack: product, architecture, code, security and the Linux servers it runs on. Most of it is TypeScript, React and Node.js on PostgreSQL, with Rust where speed matters.

I also know the other side of the screen. My family has run a distribution agency in Mankachar since 1999, and I founded a K-12 school and a charitable trust there. So when I build billing, distribution or school software, I'm building for work I do myself.

Selected work

Software I've built.

  • ZiloOne

    Self-hosted business suite

    Mail, meetings, files, office apps, invoicing, inventory, accounting, HR and CRM on one Linux server with one sign-in. Written end to end in TypeScript.

  • Propus

    Data platform

    SQL, cache, streams, object storage and search in one server, compatible with PostgreSQL, Redis, Kafka and S3, on its own storage engine.

  • Antopus

    Embedded database

    A full database in pure TypeScript: SQL, a typed ORM, documents, search, vectors and row-level security in one file, with zero dependencies.

  • Syntelix ERP

    Multi-tenant ERP

    Sales, purchasing, inventory, HR and payroll, a document drive and team chat for companies with many branches.

  • Zilo System

    GST billing & POS

    Offline-first billing for retail and wholesale: barcode checkout, multi-unit conversion, UPI QR on receipts and GST returns.

  • Finora

    Desktop accounting

    Offline accounting, inventory and payroll for Windows, with every record kept on the local PC.

Arif Hussain is a software developer and entrepreneur from Assam, India. He is the founder and CEO of BigSur Corp, the sales director of Arif Plastic Agency, the founder and a trustee of BlueCrest Royal Academy, and the founder and secretary of Abul Foundation.

What software has Arif Hussain built?
